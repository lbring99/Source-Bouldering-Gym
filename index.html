Built by climbers, for climbers

Source has been Laramie's home for indoor bouldering since it opened at 1379 N Cedar St, started by longtime local climber Davin Bagdonas and a small group of partners who wanted a dependable place to train through the winter.

Bagdonas had already run a bouldering gym above the Wagner Building downtown for five years before closing it in 2013 for grad school. When the current space became available, he and his partners rebuilt it as Source — this time with multiple owners, so the gym would outlast any one person's schedule.

It's stayed small and unpretentious on purpose: routes get reset and rebuilt by hand, membership runs on an honor-system appointment rather than a sales pitch, and the crowd is mostly the same climbers you'll see at Vedauwoo once the snow melts.
